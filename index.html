How to play

Aim — hold the left / right edges of the screen (or A / D keys).

Club — the caddie pre-selects one for the distance & wind. Swap with ‹ › (or Q / E). On the green it hands you the putter — a full stroke rolls about 1.35× the distance to the cup, and slopes break the ball.

Wedge styles (or S) — CHIP flies normal, FLOP goes high and short and sits down, BUMP flies low and runs out long.

Swing — drag down to take the club back, then push up through the ball in one motion.

  • Straight stroke → straight ball. Skew right = slice, left = hook.
  • Clean stroke (no wiggles) → full distance.
  • Pace — not too fast (low bullet), not too slow (ballooned).
  • Smooth — jerky speed sprays your aim.

Each hole, the golfer furthest from the pin is cut. Outlast everyone for the top prize.

The Wheel — win it all and you tee off at a giant spinning prize wheel. Wherever your ball ends up, the wheel decides your bonus: ×1 up to ×5 on the champion's prize.
